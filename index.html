Common Questions

How are crypto airdrops taxed by the IRS?
The IRS treats crypto airdrops as ordinary income taxed at fair market value on the date received (IRS Notice 2014-21). If the token later drops in value, you may still owe tax on the original value — known as phantom income. Securus specializes in phantom income mitigation strategies that can legally reduce or eliminate this liability.
What is phantom income in crypto taxes?
Phantom income occurs when you receive airdropped tokens at a high valuation, owe income tax on that value, but the tokens subsequently decline. You pay taxes on money you never effectively received. Our CPAs use strategies including immediate disposition elections, HIFO cost basis, and entity structuring to reduce phantom income liability.
Can I deduct crypto hack or theft losses on my taxes?
Yes. Under IRC §165(c)(2), losses from crypto hacks and theft may be deductible as casualty or theft losses. Victims of DeFi protocol hacks, bridge exploits, and exchange failures may qualify. Documentation, timing, and proper election of the loss in the correct tax year are critical — our CPAs handle this end-to-end.
What is HIFO cost basis and why does it matter?
HIFO (Highest In, First Out) is a cost basis method that sells your highest-priced crypto lots first, minimizing taxable capital gains. The IRS permits HIFO for cryptocurrency under Rev. Proc. 2024-28. Combined with tax-loss harvesting, HIFO typically reduces crypto capital gains by 20–40% compared to FIFO. Our CPAs implement this across all chains automatically.
Do I owe taxes on DeFi staking and liquidity mining rewards?
Yes — the IRS treats DeFi staking rewards and liquidity mining income as ordinary income when received. However, entity restructuring (S-Corp or partnership elections), timing strategies, and qualified business income deductions can reduce the effective rate by 30–60%. Book a consultation to see what applies to your situation.
Is wrapping ETH to wETH a taxable event?
No — wrapping ETH into wETH (or SOL into wSOL) is generally not a taxable event because you're exchanging identical economic value. However, wrapping into liquid staking tokens like stETH is treated as a deposit and may trigger income recognition when rewards accrue. Our calculator automatically flags these distinctions for your wallet.
